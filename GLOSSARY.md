# SynapseTwin Cognitive Twin & BRIAN Experience

Empathetic, continuous cognitive rhythm assessment and adaptive growth companion translating complex ML psychometrics into accessible consumer experiences.

## Language

### 1. Cognitive Architecture & State

**Personal Cognitive Twin**:
A living mathematical model that mirrors and anticipates your individual cognitive rhythms and learning pacing over time.
_Avoid_: SAT-JEPA Latent Twin State, Cognitive Digital Twin, Psychometric Predictive Agent, Latent Embedding Vector

**Your Thinking Style**:
A qualitative persona cluster representing your natural rhythm of attentional allocation, trade-off handling, and cognitive pacing.
_Avoid_: SAT-JEPA Latent Twin State & Hysteresis, GMM Archetype Cluster (k=0..8), Latent Archetype Discovery, Clustering Class

**Weekly Progress Boost**:
The measured rate of positive adaptive change in cognitive endurance, speed, and accuracy across weekly micro-sessions.
_Avoid_: Differential Vector Δc (+0.18), Delta C, Parameter Derivative, Latent Gradient

**Auto-Paced to Your Flow Zone**:
Dynamic stimulus pacing that smoothly adjusts game difficulty to sustain an optimal 85% accuracy state without cognitive fatigue.
_Avoid_: QUEST+ / Elo Psychometric Staircase Target, Adaptive Item Response Theory, Staircase Algorithm, Bayesian Target Level

### 2. Statistical Confidence & Calibration

**Clarity Range (±%)**:
The honest statistical interval reflecting where your cognitive capacity resides until more low-friction micro-sessions narrow the band.
_Avoid_: Epistemic Uncertainty (±1.96σ CI), Uncertainty Polygon Error Bands, Standard Error of Measurement, Confidence Interval Margin

**Calibration Confidence**:
A percentage indicating how thoroughly the twin understands your unique baseline rhythm across completed micro-blocks.
_Avoid_: Posterior Entropy Reduction, QUEST+ Information Gain, Convergence Metric, Fit Parameter

### 3. Explainability & Feedback

**✨ Why Brian Suggests This**:
A concise, three-bullet rationale detailing what you did, how your profile shifted, and when pacing will adjust.
_Avoid_: 5-Part Reasoning Trail (Telemetry -> Inference -> Counterfactual), Bayesian Evidence Chain, Audit DAG, Decision Trace

**Co-Pilot Synergy with Brian (0–100)**:
An exploratory index measuring how effectively a learner audits, verifies, and navigates AI assistant guidance.
_Avoid_: 0–100 AI Collaboration Score (raw title), Human-AI Alignment Index, Algorithmic Synergy Quotient

### 4. Cognitive Dimensions & Arena

**Visual Memory Clarity**:
The precision of distinguishing familiar visual cues from subtle lure variations without false alarms.
_Avoid_: Episodic Pattern Separation, Mnemonic Similarity Task (MST), Dentate Gyrus Lure Rejection

**Wide-Angle Focus**:
The capacity to rapidly register simultaneous central and peripheral visual stimuli under brief presentation.
_Avoid_: Useful Field of View (UFOV), Divided Perceptual Speed, Visual Angle Threshold

**Distraction Shield**:
The ability to maintain focus on primary targets while actively suppressing high-contrast conflicting visual clutter.
_Avoid_: Inhibitory Flanker, Eriksen Flanker Task, Stroop Interference Quotient, Incongruency Suppression Cost

**Mental Stamina**:
The sustained capacity to maintain high decision accuracy without cognitive fatigue degradation across a session.
_Avoid_: Fatigue Resistance Index, Saccadic Velocity Attenuation, Time-on-Task Performance Decay

**Memory Sharpness**:
The quickness and fidelity of encoding, updating, and recalling active patterns across short delays.
_Avoid_: N-Back Working Memory Updating, Working Memory Buffer Capacity, Retrieval Latency Drift

**Focus Consistency**:
The stability and regularity of response timing across repeated trials without hesitation spikes.
_Avoid_: Reaction Time Jitter, Intra-individual Variability, Ex-Gaussian Tau Tail, Saccadic Latency Variance

### 5. Daily Flow & Rhythm

**Daily Energy & Focus Rhythm**:
The natural daily variance in alertness and executive stamina tracked to schedule sessions during your peak windows.
_Avoid_: Continuous Saccadic Drift, Fatigue Heatmap, Circadian Oculomotor Matrix, Microsaccadic Jitter Map

**Rest-Protected Streak**:
A zero-guilt habit tracker that honors scheduled recovery days and rest pauses without penalty or streak reset.
_Avoid_: Gamified Punishment Streak, Broken Streak, Lost Streak, Penalty Reset, Compulsory Daily Streak

**Calibration Session**:
A brief, 90-to-120-second micro-round set designed to calibrate cognitive baseline without test anxiety.
_Avoid_: Cognitive Assessment Battery, Psychometric Evaluation Exam, Testing Protocol, Diagnostic Run

### 6. Companion Agents

**BRIAN**:
The friendly, empathetic axolotl companion embodying adaptive guidance, celebration, and neuroplasticity encouragement.
_Avoid_: AI Evaluator, Diagnostic Avatar, Assessment Bot, Virtual Proctor

**EMILY**:
The ambient mood and pointer rhythm calibrator that quietly measures baseline pointer pacing and room atmosphere in the background.
_Avoid_: Passive Biometric Telemetry Harvester, Motor Tremor Tracker, Keystroke Dynamics Logger
